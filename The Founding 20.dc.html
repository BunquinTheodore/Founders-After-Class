Why this exists

The best help you'll get is a person, not another playbook.

You've saved the videos. You've read the threads. But the thing that actually moved your project forward was a single honest conversation with someone a step ahead of you — someone who'd hit your exact wall and told you what they did about it.

Those conversations are rare because the right people are never in the same room. So we're building the room on purpose: twenty student builders, one table, one evening. You bring the problem you're stuck on. The table helps you think it through — and a few of them keep checking in long after.

"You don't have an information problem. You have a table problem — you've never had the right twenty people in one place."

This is the pilot batch — the first twenty. Get in now and you help shape what Founders After Class becomes.

Who's at the table

Twenty students who build, not just talk.

No headliner, no keynote, no one selling from the front. Everyone at this table is a student with something in progress — and everyone's here to help the next person move theirs forward. It's the table we wish we'd had earlier.

The filter

Paying doesn't get you in. Qualifying does.

This is not an open event. We're not filling seats — we're choosing twenty people worth learning from. You must be a student who is genuinely building: a real venture, a team or a one-person operation shipping products that actually sell, or a community you've grown yourself. Wanting in isn't enough — you have to be qualified. Every reservation is reviewed against that bar.

If you paid and don't qualify, we refund you in full. We'd rather return your ₱499 than water down the table. The whole point is a room where everyone in it has real experience to share about making money and building something that matters.
